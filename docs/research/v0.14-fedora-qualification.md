Client date: 2026-10-02, Africa/Cairo.

**The Fedora performance campaign has not started. Cross-platform Outcome B
cannot yet be confirmed.** The installed codec is zlib-ng, and the unchanged
version guard accepts its compatibility version even though the existing bound

architectural contradiction was observed. This is a qualification blocker, not
evidence to reopen the runtime architecture. No final cross-platform outcome

## Bound qualification mismatch

The frozen formula gives 1,048,903 bytes for a 1-MiB raw-deflate input. A direct
read-only call to the installed `libz.so.1` using `deflateInit2_` with levels
1/6/9, method 8, windowBits -15, memLevel 8 and strategy 0 gives
**1,114,119 bytes** from `deflateBound` for each level. Initialization and cleanup
both succeed. Node reports `node_shared_zlib=true`; its library dynamically
imports `deflate` and `deflateInit2_`.

The existing `checkZlib()` regex accepts `1.3.1.zlib-ng` because it permits a dot
after `1.3.1`. The guard therefore passes; it was neither disabled nor edited.
This compatibility label does not supply the missing derivation for zlib-ng.

The versioned [zlib-ng 2.3.3 implementation](https://github.com/zlib-ng/zlib-ng/blob/2.3.3/deflate.c#L604)
uses different bound arithmetic. Its branch without the quick strategy returns
`N + floor(N / 16) + 7` for raw default parameters, matching the installed
library's observation. That match supports the interpretation of the observed
bound; it is not a complete audit of Fedora's build options or patches.

The upstream [bound correction discussion](https://github.com/zlib-ng/zlib-ng/pull/1071)
also documents changes to worst-case output assumptions. Its linked historical
reproduction uses different parameters and is **not** a reproduction of failure
under this campaign's fixed memLevel 8. A larger native bound alone does not prove
that an output can exceed the frozen formula under the campaign settings.

The prepared bound command completed all **324 fixtures**, with every actual
compressed length at or below the unchanged formula and every round trip exact.
The new raw artifact is
[compression-v0.14-fedora-bounds.json](../../benchmarks/results/compression-v0.14-fedora-bounds.json).
An additional diagnostic examined 648 deterministic combinations of size, seed,
sparse three-byte repetition and level; none exceeded the formula. This is
qualification exploration only, not retained performance evidence or proof of
a general bound. Its generator and result are retained in validation metadata.

Continuing would require a derivation establishing the frozen maximum for this
specific implementation and parameters, or a separately authorized change to
the codec/environment or benchmark contract. This attempt makes none of those
changes. Lifecycle, performance, supplement and fresh-process memory campaigns
remain unrun; no successful campaign artifacts or measurements report are fabricated.

## Fedora-specific findings and decision

The new pain point is **Node/zlib behavior and benchmark methodology**: the guard
accepts a compatibility suffix whose bound provenance is outside the existing
derivation. Architectural owner: codec qualification and benchmark documentation.
Runtime source, the formula, guard, historical pain log and Windows reports stay
unchanged. The test reporter behavior is an additional validation-methodology
observation, not a runtime test regression.

No missing PJS primitive or justification for `@pjs/compression` was discovered.
The Fedora campaign is **blocked at codec qualification**, not complete and not
a confirmed unsafe-bound failure. Neither cross-platform Outcome B nor reopening
on a demonstrated correctness failure is justified yet. No completion commit
is created, because the required reproduction evidence is incomplete. No v0.15
work starts.
